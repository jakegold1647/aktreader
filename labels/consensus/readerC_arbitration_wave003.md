# Reader C — Blind Arbitration, Wave 003 (Serock 1890 deaths, acts 27–42)

Method: full-spread orientation pass, then 4×–12× System.Drawing (HighQualityBicubic) crops of every disputed token; all verdicts read from crops (~120 PNGs in scratchpad `readerC_wave003\`). Comparator tokens (known «девять» act 31, known «десяти» in «пятидесяти» act 31) were used to calibrate the scribe's с vs в letterforms. I did not open labels\ or coordination\.

Scribe letterform key (this hand):
- д = ∂-form: bowl with tall flag curling back LEFT over the bowl.
- б = bowl with tail sweeping up-RIGHT.
- р = straight long descender, no flag.
- с = small open e-like curve; в = closed bowl with ascender (b-form). This pair decides all девять/десять disputes.
- т = m-form; п = n-form.

## No-vote observations (ink neither candidate set captured)

1. **Act 40 struck filiation**: beneath the red «дочь Гутмана и Естеры» the struck black reads «сынъ [О/Э]к[н]я(?) и [?]оды(?)» — I cannot resolve the two struck parent-names; flagging rather than guessing. The red mother-name reads «Естеры» (Эстеры).
2. **Act 40 correction anatomy**: original black = «умеръ Гутманъ Бодекъ… сынъ …»; red corrector overwrote -ла onto «умеръ», struck «Гутманъ», interlined «Элька», struck «сынъ …», interlined «дочь Гутмана и Естеры» — and left the closing «о кончинѣ Гутмана Бодекъ» untouched. The child appears to have been first registered under her father's name.
3. **Standing witness (item 20)**: in acts 35 and 39 Колтунъ's occupation is «кладбищ(ен)ный служитель» (cemetery attendant) — neither «работникъ» nor «рыбакъ». In act 39 his age also reads «шестидесяти вос[ьми]» (68?) against the usual «шестидесяти пяти» (65). Recommend a dedicated pass.
4. **Act 33 declarant age**: first element genuinely ambiguous «двадцати/тридцати»; if «тридцати», the true age is 36 — outside both offered candidates. Second element «шести» is firm.
5. **Act 30 occupation tail**: the word wraps as «смолю(?)=/щаго(?)» before «шестидесяти семи лѣтъ» — possibly a participial spelling of the tar-maker trade; the stem «смол-» is the only firm part.
6. **Act 34 given name**: medial letters smudged; «Байля» is a live alternative to «Бейля».
7. **Act 39 opening hour**: the scribe omitted «въ» («года восемь часовъ утра») — worth noting since the 7-o'clock reading depends on parsing the в as «въ».
8. **Act 29 (not in dispute list)** repeats «Штатзингеръ» twice (deceased «Добра Ита Штатзингеръ», filiation) — same з letterform; corroborates item 10 if ever needed.
