## Q1 — Act 1, day-word after «Января»

**Verdict: SINGLE** · **Confidence: SURE**

Transcription: `Состоялось въ посадѣ Сероцкъ Января перваго дня тысяча восемьсотъ девяностаго года въ одиннадцать часовъ утра.`

Reasoning: the line runs «Января перваго дня» straight into the line-end with no parenthesis, no bracket, no superscript and no second numeral-word anywhere on line 1 or at the head of line 2 (line 2 opens directly with «тысяча восемьсотъ девяностаго года»); this register uses the single Julian day only.

## Q2 — Act 1, deceased's given name

**Verdict: «Рухля»** · **Confidence: SURE**

Transcription: `умерла Рухля Малованьчикъ`; closing repeats `о кончинѣ Рухли Малованьчикъ`.

Reasoning: after the ornamental capital there is exactly ONE long descender loop (у) followed by х–л and a terminal я — «Фрума» would require two consecutive descenders (р then у) and end in а/ы, and the genitive in the closing formula ends in a plain single-arch «и» (Рухли), not «ы» (Фрумы).

## Q3 — Act 1, mother's given name

**Verdict: «Рифки»** · **Confidence: SURE**

Transcription: `дочь покойныхъ родителей Абрама и Рифки супруговъ`

Reasoning: the initial capital is the same oval-with-left-descending-curl that the scribe uses for Р in «Рухля/Рухли»; the letter immediately after it is a short arch «и» with no descender (ruling out Ц's right-hand tail construction), and after the unmistakable ф comes к + и, not р + ы.

## Q4 — Act 1, parents' surname after «супруговъ»

**Verdict: «Антошевичъ»** · **Confidence: SURE** · cross-reference MATCHES act 5

Transcription: `супруговъ Антошевичъ`; act 5 (scan 2, upper right): `урожденной Антошевичъ`

Reasoning: the initial is the scribe's looped capital А (open bowl + right leg), not the tall single-stroke Л, and the medial cluster is т-о-ш-е (three-arch ш), not н-о-в; letterform-for-letterform the act 1 and act 5 occurrences are the same word by the same hand — same capital А, same ш, same terminal -вичъ.

## Q5 — Act 1, surviving husband's name after «мужа»

**Verdict: «Цука»** · **Confidence: LEANING**

Transcription: `Оставивъ послѣ себя мужа Цука.`

Reasoning: the body is unambiguously -у-к-а (one descender loop shaped exactly like the у of «Рухли» on the next line, then к, then а), which eliminates «Юдка» (no д oval) and «Шука» (capital has two vertical elements plus a lower-left tail, not Ш's three); the capital's large left flourish loop is the only residual doubt, hence LEANING rather than SURE.

## Q6 — Act 1, words between «Израель» and «пятидесяти пяти»

**Verdict: (a) «Іосковичъ фельдшеръ»** · **Confidence: SURE**

Transcription: `Явился лично Израель Іосковичъ фельдшеръ пятидесяти пяти лѣтъ и Янкель Бренштейнъ школьникъ сорока пять лѣтъ…`

Signature check: the first signature below the act reads «Іосковичъ» — a tall J/І flourish with descender followed by -осковичъ; it is nowhere near «Гольдфарбъ» (no Г, no л-д-ф cluster, no бъ terminal). The second signature is «Я. Бренштейнъ».

Reasoning: the second word begins with the same І-flourish as the signature and reads Іосковичъ; the third word is фельдшеръ (ф with loops above and below the line, ь, then -шеръ), which supplies the occupation the formula requires — under reading (b) no other word in the span could serve as the occupation, so (b) would leave the formula defective.

## Q7 — Act 2, day-word after «Января»

**Verdict: «восемьнадцатаго» (SINGLE, 18th)** · **Confidence: SURE**

Transcription: `Состоялось въ посадъ Сероцкъ Января восемьнадцатаго дня тысяча восемьсотъ девяностаго года въ десять часовъ утра.`

Reasoning: one continuous word with the -надцат- infix clearly formed (н-а-д-ц-а-т); there is no slash, no second day-word and no parenthetical, and the following word is «дня» directly.

## Q8 — Act 2, deceased infant's surname

**Verdict: «Аукштукальская»** · **Confidence: SURE** · cross-reference: same stem as act 3, different initial

Transcription: `умерла Хана Аукштукаль-|ская девять мѣсяцевъ отъ роду`; closing repeats `о кончинѣ Хаи Аукштукальской`.

Reasoning: the word is hyphen-broken at the line end and its tail «ская» is carried to the following line, and the closing formula gives the unambiguous feminine adjectival genitive «Аукштукальской» — so the surname is adjectival, not the bare stem «Аукштукъ»; act 3's first declarant «Лейбъ Укштукальскій портной» shows the identical -укальск- letter chain with the masculine -ій ending, differing only in lacking the initial А (a scribal variant worth flagging to the indexer).

## Q9 — Act 2, mother's maiden name after «урожденной»

**Verdict: «Конколь»** · **Confidence: SURE**

Transcription: `дочь Лейбы и Маи урожденной Конколь`

Reasoning: К-о-н-к-о-л-ь — both vowels are fully closed round о of the same size, the fourth letter is a second к with its distinct diagonal arm (not р, which would carry a descender), and the word ends л + ь; «Конкаль» would need an open-topped а in position 5 and «Корки» has no terminal soft sign.
