# Data license note

This note was previously appended to the MIT license text in `LICENSE`. It is not part of the MIT grant, which covers the code in this repository.

The `gold/`, `labels/`, and `resources/` directories contain transcriptions and
derived structured records from 19th-century civil registers held by the Polish
State Archives (Archiwum Państwowe w Warszawie, zespół 73/826/0, Serock), whose
scans are in the public domain. Those transcriptions and derived records are
released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).

All persons appearing in these records are deceased and the records are more
than a century old. No living person's data is included in this repository.

Scan images themselves are not redistributed here; the repository records their
SHA-256 hashes and their public source locations so results can be reproduced.
